# Code of conduct

Rusted is a small project and everyone working on it, or just asking questions, should feel
welcome here.

## Expected
- Be patient and kind, especially with people new to Rust or to Minecraft servers.
- Keep feedback about the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask before jumping to conclusions.

## Not okay
- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Trolling or derailing issues and pull requests on purpose.

## Reporting
Open a [private report](https://github.com/RustedMC/Rusted/security/advisories/new) or
message [@RustedMC](https://github.com/RustedMC) on GitHub. Reports are kept private.

Depending on what happened, the response can be a warning, removed comments, or a ban from
the repository.

Based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
